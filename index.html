GETTING STARTED2 MIN TO SETUP

How to use NOVA — 4 steps.

You fly. NOVA helps. No wiring into your plane, no fiddly servers — just a copilot that reads the sky and hands you the plan.

STEP 1

Open the app

  1. Double-click start.cmd — NOVA boots a local server and opens itself in your browser.
  2. Keep it in a second window next to MSFS 2024.

◈ No install, no plugins, no injected mods into your sim.

STEP 2

Sign in

  1. Google or X (Twitter) — one click, no password.
  2. You’re remembered on this device, so it’s one click from then on.

◈ Your data stays yours — accounts live on your own server.

STEP 3

Build your flight

  1. Pick origin + destination — NOVA reads live VATSIM traffic, weather, wind and charts.
  2. Get a wind-optimal route plus SID/STAR and approach, all in one screen.

◈ Type on the AI copilot tab to ask anything about your flight.
